Our mission

The National Club Guild is a concept for a cross-school network where student organizations can discover one another, exchange practical resources, share events, and help new clubs get started.

We believe students can create stronger communities when they can learn from people outside their own club or school.

How it works

Connect. Discover clubs by interest, category, and school.

Create. Use guides and templates to plan projects and register organizations.

Lead. Share opportunities, learn from other leaders, and build sustainable teams.

Community principles

  • Respect every club's independence and goals.
  • Share credit and collaborate fairly.
  • Keep personal information private.
  • Follow school policies and local requirements.
  • Make participation welcoming and accessible.

Before launching publicly

Appoint trusted moderators, write community rules, verify club listings, and decide how reports and membership requests will be handled. Do not publish student contact information without clear permission.
